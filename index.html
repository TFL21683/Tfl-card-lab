Foil Bowing & C/S Curve
1. Holo / Reverse Bowing

Correctable: 95%-100% flat via RH-controlled chamber (65–70%) and weighted clamp cycles.

Scratches & Surface Haze
2. Gloss & Holo Scratches

Correctable: 50%-80% smoothed using coat-safe micro-abrasive polymer pads.
Uncorrectable: Deep gouges piercing the foil layer or factory SAR texture cuts.

Edge Chipping & Whitening
3. Edges & Corner Whitening

Correctable: 70%-90% lifted via precision micro-vapor hydration and stylus tooling.
Uncorrectable: Complete loss of printed cardstock core or heavy paper fraying.

Core Crease & Color Break
4. Deep Crease (Color Broken)

Correctable: 30%-50% mitigated via multi-stage hydration and high-pressure clamping.
Uncorrectable: Inner paper core split is permanent; a faint white fracture line remains visible.
