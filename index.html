Holo Scratches & Haze
1. Surface & Holo Scratches

Correctable: Light hairline swirls and gloss scuffs (50%-80% smoothed via polymer buffing pad).
Uncorrectable: Deep gouges piercing the foil layer or factory SAR texture cuts.

Edge Whitening / Silvering
2. Edges & Corner Whitening

Correctable: Minor fiber fraying or light border smoothing.
Uncorrectable: Complete loss of printed cardstock core or heavy chips exposing white paper layers.

Crease Analysis
3. Creases & Bends

Correctable: Surface wrinkles with intact color ink (60%-85% mitigated via thermal press).
Uncorrectable: Deep creases with broken color/split paper cores (Max 30%-50% recovery; white line remains permanent).
